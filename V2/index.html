Market-wide views from the floorsheet archive. Same data as the v1 dashboard, different lens: whole-market patterns instead of one stock or broker. No backend — data is fetched directly from GitHub and cached in your browser.

Churn scatter

One bubble per broker-stock pair. X = buy amount, Y = sell amount (log scale). Points on the diagonal are round-trips (churn); far from it = directional flow. net cash out (buying) net cash in (selling) Bubble size = contracts.

Accumulation / distribution streaks

Broker-stock pairs ranked by consecutive same-sign days in range. The strip shows each day: green = net buy, red = net sell, dark = no trade.

Activity leadership by day

Daily rank by gross turnover (rank 1 = most active). Lines show how leadership rotates between TMS members.

Data source: Summary/ · (cache is shared with the v1 dashboard)

net_qty is a daily flow; streaks and heatmaps are aggregates of it, not holdings. A broker (TMS) aggregates many clients — accumulation here is a proxy, not one investor. net_cash = sell − buy: positive means net cash received (selling). Gross turnover is two-sided per broker; do not sum it across brokers and call it market turnover.
